## OpenMan runs autonomous agents that take real actions

OpenMan gives an AI agent a cloud computer and the accounts you connect, then lets it work without asking you before
every step. Depending on how you set it up, the agent can:

- run code and shell commands, install software, and change or delete files on its computer;
- browse the web, sign in to sites, fill in forms and submit them;
- send email, text messages and chat messages, and place or answer phone calls, in your name or the agent's;
- use connected apps (for example email, calendars, documents, code hosting, CRMs) with the permissions you granted;
- spend money: Orgo credits for compute and models, and charges from any service you connect that bills per use,
  such as phone numbers, messages, speech, image or video generation.

Approvals, usage caps and other safeguards in OpenMan reduce risk. They do not remove it.

## AI output may be wrong

The agent uses AI models that can be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or inappropriate, and can misread
instructions or web pages, including pages written to mislead it (prompt injection). Do not treat its output as
professional advice (legal, medical, financial or otherwise), and check anything that matters.
