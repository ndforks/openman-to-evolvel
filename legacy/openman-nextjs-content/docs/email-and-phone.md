# Email and phone

> **Coming soon.** Agent email and agent phone are built, but they stay off until the email and phone providers are connected. Until then the **Mail** and **Phone** settings report that they are not set up, and none of what follows works yet. This page will be filled in once they go live.

## What to expect

- **Agent email**: the agent has an email address of its own. Messages from senders you approve can open or continue a task; messages from anyone else wait for your review.
- **Agent phone**: the agent has its own US or Canadian number. Texts from approved numbers open or continue a task, and the answer comes back as a text. The agent checks with you before it sends one.

AgentMail and AgentPhone will provide these services, and the [privacy policy](/privacy) will name them once they are switched on.
