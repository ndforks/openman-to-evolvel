# Overview

{{name}} is an open-source AI agent with a cloud computer to work on. Tell it what you need; it makes a plan, uses a browser, a terminal and files on that computer, and carries on until the job is finished or it has a question for you. The computer's screen streams into the app, so you can watch, and you can take the controls whenever you like.

{{name}} runs on Orgo. Your Orgo account is your sign-in, the agent works on a cloud computer in your Orgo workspace, and everything it uses is paid from your Orgo credits at Orgo's rates. {{name}} has no subscription of its own.

## The pieces underneath

- **An Orgo account.** Signing in links {{name}} to your existing Orgo account, or makes a new one. See [Sign in with Orgo](/docs/getting-started).
- **A cloud computer.** The first time you sign in, {{name}} prepares a home computer in your Orgo workspace and installs Hermes Agent on it. Tasks run there. See [Cloud computers](/docs/cloud-computers).
- **Claude, through Orgo.** The agent thinks with Anthropic's Claude models, reached through Orgo's model proxy and charged to your credits. See [Modes and models](/docs/modes-and-models).
- **Orgo credits.** A credit equals one US dollar. See [Credits and billing](/docs/credits-and-billing).
