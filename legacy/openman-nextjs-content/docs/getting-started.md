# Sign in with Orgo

{{name}} does not keep accounts of its own. You sign in with Orgo, and your work uses that account's cloud computers, credits and plan.

## Signing in

1. Choose **Sign in** or **Get started** anywhere on the site, then pick any option in the dialog that opens.
2. orgo.ai opens in a new tab and asks you to approve access. No Orgo account yet? You can create one right there.
3. Approve, then switch back to the {{name}} tab. It picks up the approval and finishes signing you in.

The approval screen on orgo.ai talks about Orgo's command-line tool. That is normal: {{name}} reuses the same approval step.

> During the invite-only period, a sign-in from an address that is not on the invite list lands on a waitlist page and is signed out straight away. Nothing from that attempt is stored.

## The first time you sign in

- {{name}} saves an encrypted key for your Orgo account, so automations and other background jobs can keep working while you are away. See [Security and data](/docs/security).
- It prepares your **home computer**: an Orgo cloud computer in your workspace with Hermes Agent installed and set to use Claude through Orgo. Expect this to take a few minutes once; after that, tasks run there.

## Running your first task

1. On the home screen, write what you need in the input box, for example *"Find three recent articles about heat pumps and summarize them in a table"*.
2. Choose a mode if you want a different model (see [Modes and models](/docs/modes-and-models)), then send.
3. The task view opens with the agent's plan, each step as it happens, and a live view of the computer. See [Tasks and the computer](/docs/tasks).

## Signing out

Pick **Sign out** from the account menu to end the session in this browser. The stored Orgo key stays, so automations keep running; to remove it, delete your {{name}} data under **Settings** (see [Security and data](/docs/security)).

## Any device with a browser

{{name}} lives in the browser. Nothing needs installing: open it in an up-to-date browser on a laptop, tablet or phone.
