# Knowledge and personalization

The agent learns about you from two places, both found under **Personalization** in the app.

## Profile

| Field | Maximum length |
|---|---|
| Nickname | 50 characters |
| Occupation | 100 characters |
| About you | 2,000 characters |
| Custom instructions | 3,000 characters |

Your profile goes into every task, and a copy is kept in the agent's user notes on your home computer so it is available there as well.

## Knowledge entries

Knowledge entries are facts or rules the agent follows in every task, for example *"Our fiscal year starts in April"* or *"Always format dates as YYYY-MM-DD"*.

- An entry has a name, its content and, optionally, a note on when it applies.
- Entries can be switched off without being deleted.
- Up to 50 entries are allowed.
- The **Use knowledge** switch enables or disables all of them together.

## Suggested entries

At the end of a task the agent may propose up to two knowledge entries drawn from what it learned. They are only saved if you accept them.

## Bringing memory from another assistant

Paste in what another AI assistant has stored about you (many can export their memory as text). {{name}} turns it into suggested profile fields and knowledge entries, and you pick which to keep. Nothing is saved until you accept.
