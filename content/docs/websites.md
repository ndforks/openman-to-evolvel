# Websites

Ask the agent for a website and it builds one on your cloud computer, shows you a preview, and can put it online at a public address.

## Building a site

Start with a task such as *"Build a one-page site for my bakery with opening hours and a contact form"*. The site is kept in a folder on your computer under version control, so no change is ever lost.

The **Websites** page in the app lists your sites. Each one has a dashboard with its preview, version history and settings, and **Edit in new task** opens a task for changes.

## Preview

During work, the preview shows the site running on your computer through an Orgo preview address. It is available whenever the computer is on.

## Publishing

**Publish** builds the site and serves it from your computer at a public address on `web.orgo.dev`. It stays up as long as that computer is running. A computer can hold up to {{urls.perComputer}} public addresses.

## Visibility

| Setting | Who can open the site |
|---|---|
| Public | Anyone who has the link |
| Invite | People you invite through Orgo |
| Private | You and your Orgo workspace only |

## Versions

Each saved change becomes a checkpoint with a screenshot. **Restore** turns an older version into a new one, so history is never overwritten.

## Downloading and copying

Download a site's files as a `.tar.gz` archive, or choose **Make a copy** to start a new site based on it.
