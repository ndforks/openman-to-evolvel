# Credits and billing

{{name}} does not bill you directly. Everything is charged to your Orgo account, at Orgo's prices.

## Credits

A credit is one US dollar of balance on your Orgo account. Credits cover:

- **Model use**: the provider's list price plus {{credits.markup}}, for every task and every automation run.
- **Hourly computers**: a Medium Linux computer, for example, costs {{medium.hourly}} per hour while it is running.

The app shows your balance. **Settings → Usage & Billing** breaks down your spending over the last 7, 30, 90 or 365 days and lists recent transactions.

## Topping up

In Usage & Billing, choose **Add credits**, enter an amount and pay through Stripe Checkout. Any whole-dollar amount between {{credits.topUpMin}} and {{credits.topUpMax}} is accepted. The credit goes to your Orgo account and can be spent immediately in {{name}} or in Orgo.

## Plans

Plans belong to Orgo and are optional. Each includes always-on computer capacity and a monthly credit top-up.

Paying yearly saves {{yearly.discount}}. [Pricing](/pricing) lists what each plan contains.

To switch plans, choose **Upgrade** in the app; Orgo's billing page opens and handles the change and payment. **Manage billing** takes you to Orgo's Stripe portal for invoices, payment methods and cancelling.
